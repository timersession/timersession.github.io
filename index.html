Daily work
Money & numbers

Start the timer — we do the math

Press Start and TimerSession counts the session time and your income for you. Switch to the small window when you want it out of the way.

  • Start, pause and stop with one click
  • Pick the client before you begin
  • Jump to Records, Analytics, Finance or Clients from the main window
  • Shrink the timer to a small always-visible window
  • Elapsed time also shown on the tray icon

Session reminders

  • Up to three reminders: set the time in minutes or tap 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120
  • Sound when the time comes: a built-in sound, or your own sound or music file — with its own volume
  • No sound needed? Show only a message — “time is up” or any text you write

Every client has their own rate and currency

Keep all your clients in one list. Set the hourly rate and currency once, and every session is priced correctly.

  • Add a client, edit the rate, or delete a client
  • Rate and currency saved for each person ($, €)
  • See how many sessions each client has had
  • Add notes, like “Weekly, Tuesday morning”
  • Search by name or surname
  • Currencies: $, €, UAH, ₽ — or add your own, for example ¥

See exactly what you earn Pro

Every session lands in one table with its date, time, client, length, rate and income. A summary on top shows the big picture for the period you choose.

  • Summary: sessions, hours, income, average income per session, clients
  • Search by name and filter by period (week, month and more)
  • Add a record by hand, delete it, or double-click any cell to edit
  • Works with several currencies at once ($ and €)
  • Export to Excel with one button

Know how busy you really are Pro

Pick a period and see your visits, total time, average session and number of unique clients. The table below keeps every visit with your notes.

  • Visits, total time, average session and unique clients
  • Choose the period: week, month and more
  • Edit any cell with a double-click and add notes
  • Add an appointment or delete a record
  • Open the Chart, or Export to Excel

Watch your revenue, time and visits by client Pro

Open the Chart from Analytics and see at a glance which clients bring the most income, take the most time or visit most often.

  • Switch between Revenue, Time and Visits
  • Pick the period: one day, a month, or any date range you need
  • Everything for the period in detail: earnings, time and number of sessions per client, with totals below — clients, visits, total time and revenue
  • Choose the currency and the period
  • Each bar shows hours × rate, so the numbers are easy to check

Data safety

Your data is yours — and it’s backed up

🔒 Stored only on your computer — never uploaded to a server📴 Timer, clients and records work fully offline

Keep your data safe with one-click backup

Save everything — sessions, clients, schedule and settings — into one ZIP file. Put it on a USB drive or in the cloud, and bring it all back whenever you need.

  1. Save backup to file… stores your whole database in a single ZIP file
  2. Reinstalled the program or moved to a new computer? Click Load backup from file… and your data returns
  3. Before loading, the program shows what is inside the copy and asks you to confirm. Your current data is saved separately first, so nothing is lost
  4. Restart the program after loading a backup
  • The license key is not included — enter it again after reinstalling
  • The copy contains your Telegram bot token, so keep the file as safe as a password

Tip: make a backup once a week and before updating the program.

FAQ

Common Questions

What operating systems does TimerSession support?+
TimerSession is a Windows desktop application. It runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11.
Can the timer stay on screen while I work?+
Yes. Switch to the compact window — a small timer that stays visible without covering your work. You can also choose a larger window size in Settings.
What languages is TimerSession available in?+
TimerSession is currently available in English, Spanish, Russian, and Ukrainian. More languages are coming soon — we're actively expanding language support.
Do I need an internet connection?+
No. Timer, clients, and records work fully offline. Internet is only needed for Google Calendar sync and the Telegram bot.
What can the Telegram bot do?+
Connect your own bot in Settings, then ask it for today's and tomorrow's appointments, your clients, the current session status, and analytics — or add and delete records right from the chat.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?+
Yes, cancel anytime. Your Pro access continues until the end of the billing period. No hidden fees.
Which plan should I choose?+
Monthly ($9.99/mo) is best if you want flexibility with no commitment. Annual ($49/yr) is the most popular — you save over 59% and it works out to just $4.08/month. Lifetime ($99 once) is the best deal if you plan to use TimerSession for 2+ years — you pay once and never worry about billing again.
Is my client data private?+
Yes. All data is stored locally on your computer. We do not upload your client information to any server.
