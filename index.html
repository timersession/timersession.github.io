FAQ

Common Questions

What operating systems does TimerSession support?+
TimerSession is a Windows desktop application. It runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11.
Can the timer stay on screen while I work?+
Yes. Switch to the compact window — a small timer that stays visible without covering your work. You can also choose a larger window size in Settings.
What languages is TimerSession available in?+
TimerSession is currently available in English, Spanish, Russian, and Ukrainian. More languages are coming soon — we're actively expanding language support.
Do I need an internet connection?+
No. Timer, clients, and records work fully offline. Internet is only needed for Google Calendar sync and the Telegram bot.
What can the Telegram bot do?+
Connect your own bot in Settings, then ask it for today's and tomorrow's appointments, your clients, the current session status, and analytics — or add and delete records right from the chat.
How does the 31-day trial work?+
Download the app and use the timer, clients, appointments, Analytics and Finance for 31 days — no credit card required. After the trial, the timer, clients, and appointments stay free. Upgrade to Pro to keep Analytics and Finance and to use the Telegram bot and Google Calendar sync, which are available with Pro only.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?+
Yes, cancel anytime. Your Pro access continues until the end of the billing period. No hidden fees.
Which plan should I choose?+
Monthly ($9.99/mo) is best if you want flexibility with no commitment. Annual ($49/yr) is the most popular — you save over 59% and it works out to just $4.08/month. Lifetime ($99 once) is the best deal if you plan to use TimerSession for 2+ years — you pay once and never worry about billing again.
Is my client data private?+
Yes. All data is stored locally on your computer. We do not upload your client information to any server.
