Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Short version: If you're not happy with TimerSession, contact us within the refund window and we'll make it right.

Refund Windows

  • Monthly subscription ($9.99/month) — 14-day refund from the date of purchase.
  • Annual subscription ($49/year) — 14-day refund from the date of purchase.
  • Lifetime license ($99) — 30-day refund from the date of purchase.

How to Request a Refund

Send an email to timersession@proton.me with:

  • The email address used at purchase
  • Your order number (found in your Paddle receipt)
  • A brief reason for the refund (optional, but helps us improve)

We will process your refund within 5 business days.

Conditions

  • Refunds are only available within the windows listed above.
  • Refunds for subscriptions cancelled after the refund window are not available for the remaining period.
  • We reserve the right to decline refund requests that show evidence of abuse (e.g. repeated purchase and refund cycles).

Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your Monthly or Annual subscription at any time via your Paddle customer portal. Cancellation stops future billing; you retain access until the end of the paid period.

Questions

Contact us at timersession@proton.me — we're happy to help.
